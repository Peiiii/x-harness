# Built-in call-frequency observation

Normal `createXGrowthHarness({tab|app,ledgerPath,proofDirectory})` bindings automatically log external business calls and browser-facade commands. No extension, timer, extra platform request or new installation is needed. Existing bindings must be rebuilt normally to adopt a newer artifact; they are not hot-updated. Lower-level diagnostic factories do not imply root-call coverage.

Files live at `proofDirectory/usage/<sessionId>/<callId>.json`. Each call starts with an unfinished record and atomically replaces it on completion. Independent session directories avoid cross-executor locks. Construction performs no filesystem or browser I/O. Records contain only identity, operation, layer, real wall-clock times, duration, result status, diagnostic code and explicit429 signal; no arguments, URLs, account names, bodies, cookies or raw exceptions. Disk errors never repeat or change a business action. `x.usageStatus()` returns local calls/failedWrites/directory; check failedWrites when diagnosing missing coverage.

Creator collection also writes nested `creator:*` step records before and after navigation, readiness, page reads and scrolling. In the report, these appear separately under `creatorSteps`/`stepRecords`, and never increase business-call or browser-command frequency. An unfinished step identifies the last started operation, not proof that the underlying host or platform caused the stall; `reason:timeout` and `connection-failed` are sanitized classifications. The trace stores no arguments, page content or raw errors.

For offline analysis, import `readXUsageReport` from `@nextclaw/x-harness/diagnostics` and call `readXUsageReport([proofDirectory],{since:'2026-10-21T16:00:00.000Z',until:'2026-10-22T16:00:00.000Z'})`. Use actual proof directories and UTC boundaries for the desired local day. Replay deduplicates IDs, reports operations/minutes/statuses/duration/unfinished/gaps, and never treats absent logs as idle traffic.

Business calls and browser commands are two layers, not HTTP requests; do not add them. Internal batch steps are represented by one external batch invocation plus browser commands. Browser commands may reuse observations or perform several internal steps. Browser background polling, manual browsing, other profiles, unlisted directories and old bindings are unknown. http429 counts explicit returned reason/error signals; it does not enumerate all network responses. Existing bounded `readXNetworkDiagnostics` can supply actual quota/429 samples during active work when needed.

Compare intensity, unsuccessful outcomes and verified ledger output in matching time windows. A lower-failure intensity is a candidate operating range, not a guaranteed rate ceiling. Do not deliberately increase traffic to discover a hidden limit. One real day may reveal patterns but cannot guarantee the precise limit or eliminate throttling.
